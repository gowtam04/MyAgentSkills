# Meridian Partners Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview

**Meridian Partners** is a B2B strategy consultancy focused on helping mid-market companies achieve sustainable growth. Our brand identity reflects strategic precision, trusted expertise, and forward-thinking leadership.

### Brand Promise
We guide mid-market companies to their true north — unlocking growth through rigorous strategy and hands-on partnership.

### Brand Personality
- **Professional** — We maintain the highest standards of corporate excellence
- **Authoritative** — Our expertise is deep and our counsel is trusted
- **Strategic** — Every decision is intentional, every move is purposeful
- **Approachable** — Despite our expertise, we are collaborative partners, not distant advisors

---

## Logo

### Primary Mark
The Meridian Partners logo consists of an abstract compass rose — a geometric mark that symbolizes strategic direction, precision, and guiding clients toward growth. The compass motif reflects our role as navigators in the complex landscape of business strategy.

### Logo Variations
1. **Primary Logo (Icon Only)** — `logo.png` (1024x1024) — The abstract compass mark alone, used when the brand name appears elsewhere or in contexts where the mark stands alone (app icons, favicons, watermarks).
2. **Logo with Wordmark** — `logo-with-text.png` (2048x1024) — The compass mark paired with "Meridian Partners" in a serif typeface, used for primary brand identification on documents, presentations, and websites.
3. **Favicon** — `favicon.png` and size variants — A simplified compass point for browser tabs and small-format use.
4. **Social Profile** — `social-profile.png` (512x512) — The compass mark on a navy background, optimized for circular cropping on social platforms.

### Logo Clear Space
Maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of one compass point (approximately 25% of the logo height). No other graphic elements, text, or borders should intrude into this space.

### Logo Minimum Size
- **Digital:** No smaller than 32px height
- **Print:** No smaller than 0.5 inches (12.7mm) height

### Logo Don'ts
- Do not stretch, skew, or distort the logo
- Do not change the logo colors outside the approved palette
- Do not add drop shadows, outlines, or other effects
- Do not place the logo on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast
- Do not rotate the logo
- Do not rearrange the compass mark relative to the wordmark

## Typography

### Primary Typeface — Headlines & Display
**Font Family:** Playfair Display (Google Fonts) or equivalent premium serif
- **Weight:** Bold (700) for headlines, Regular (400) for subheadings
- **Usage:** Headlines, titles, pull quotes, the "Meridian Partners" wordmark
- **Character:** Elegant, authoritative, refined

**Fallback stack:** `"Playfair Display", Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif`

### Secondary Typeface — Body & UI
**Font Family:** Inter (Google Fonts) or equivalent clean sans-serif
- **Weight:** Regular (400) for body, Medium (500) for emphasis, Semibold (600) for labels
- **Usage:** Body copy, navigation, buttons, form fields, captions
- **Character:** Clean, modern, highly readable

**Fallback stack:** `"Inter", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`

### Type Scale

| Element | Font | Size (Desktop) | Size (Mobile) | Weight | Line Height | Letter Spacing |
|---------|------|----------------|---------------|--------|-------------|----------------|
| H1 | Playfair Display | 48px / 3rem | 32px / 2rem | 700 | 1.2 | -0.02em |
| H2 | Playfair Display | 36px / 2.25rem | 28px / 1.75rem | 700 | 1.25 | -0.01em |
| H3 | Playfair Display | 28px / 1.75rem | 22px / 1.375rem | 400 | 1.3 | 0 |
| H4 | Inter | 20px / 1.25rem | 18px / 1.125rem | 600 | 1.4 | 0 |
| Body | Inter | 16px / 1rem | 16px / 1rem | 400 | 1.6 | 0 |
| Body Small | Inter | 14px / 0.875rem | 14px / 0.875rem | 400 | 1.5 | 0 |
| Caption | Inter | 12px / 0.75rem | 12px / 0.75rem | 400 | 1.4 | 0.02em |
| Button | Inter | 14px / 0.875rem | 14px / 0.875rem | 600 | 1 | 0.05em |

### Typography Guidelines
- Always use sentence case for body copy
- Use title case for headlines and navigation items
- Maximum line length: 65-75 characters for optimal readability
- Paragraph spacing: 1.5x the body line height

## Business Card

### Specifications
- **Size:** 3.5" x 2" (standard US business card)
- **Paper:** 16pt matte or soft-touch laminate recommended
- **Finish:** Gold foil stamp on compass mark (optional premium upgrade)

### Front Layout (`business-card-front.png`)
- White background
- Compass mark centered in upper portion (navy + gold)
- "Meridian Partners" in Playfair Display below the mark
- Name and title in Inter Medium
- Contact details in Inter Regular
- Thin gold accent line as divider

### Back Layout (`business-card-back.png`)
- Navy blue background
- Subtle compass watermark in slightly lighter navy
- Thin gold border line
- Clean and minimal — no text

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Attributes
- **Confident, not arrogant** — We share expertise without condescension
- **Clear, not simplistic** — We make complex ideas accessible without dumbing them down
- **Strategic, not jargon-heavy** — We use precise language, not buzzwords
- **Warm, not casual** — We are human and approachable while maintaining professionalism

### Writing Guidelines
- Lead with insight, not with selling
- Use active voice
- Keep sentences concise (aim for 15-20 words average)
- Avoid industry jargon unless writing for a specialized audience
- Always tie recommendations back to business outcomes

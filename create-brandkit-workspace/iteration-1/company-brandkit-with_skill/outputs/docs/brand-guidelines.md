# Meridian Partners Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview

Meridian Partners is a B2B strategy consultancy focused on helping mid-market companies unlock sustainable growth. The brand embodies strategic clarity, trusted expertise, and forward momentum — qualities captured in the compass rose mark that serves as the firm's visual anchor. Every brand touchpoint should communicate authority, precision, and partnership.

## Brand Values

- **Strategic Clarity:** We cut through complexity to reveal the path forward. Our brand reflects this through clean design, purposeful composition, and deliberate color choices.
- **Trusted Partnership:** We are invested in our clients' success. The brand conveys reliability, professionalism, and long-term commitment.
- **Directional Leadership:** Like a compass, we help organizations orient toward their goals. The brand communicates confidence and forward momentum.
- **Disciplined Excellence:** We hold ourselves to the highest standards. Every detail of the brand — from logo spacing to color application — reflects this discipline.

## Logo

### Primary Logo
The primary logo is an abstract compass rose rendered in navy blue and gold. The compass symbolizes strategic direction and guidance — core to Meridian Partners' mission of helping mid-market companies navigate growth. The geometric angularity of the mark conveys precision and analytical rigor, while the gold accents add warmth and a premium quality.

Use the primary logo in most contexts on light or white backgrounds.

### Logo Variations

### Logo Usage Rules
- Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the compass mark's center circle
- Minimum display size: 32px height for digital, 0.5" for print
- Do not stretch, rotate, or distort the logo
- Do not change the logo colors outside the approved palette
- Do not place on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast
- Do not add effects (drop shadows, outlines, gradients, bevels)
- When placing on colored backgrounds, use only the reversed (white) version on dark backgrounds or the primary version on light/white backgrounds
- Never place the full-color logo on a gold or navy background — use the reversed version instead

## Voice & Tone

- **Tone:** Confident and professional, but not stiff. Knowledgeable without being condescending. Authoritative yet approachable — the voice of a trusted advisor.
- **Writing style:** Clear, concise, and direct. Favor active voice. Use data and evidence to support claims. Prefer specificity over generality.
- **Language level:** Executive-level business language. Comfortable with strategy terminology but always explains concepts when writing for broader audiences.
- **Avoid:** Jargon without explanation, overly casual language, hyperbole, buzzwords without substance (e.g., "synergy," "paradigm shift" without context), and hedging language that undermines authority.

## Brand Applications

### Digital
- Website headers and footers should use the primary logo on white/light backgrounds
- Email signatures should feature the icon mark at 48px with the company name in Playfair Display
- Social media profile pictures use the icon mark; cover images use the branded gradient backgrounds provided

### Print
- Business cards use the navy and gold palette with the compass mark
- Letterheads feature the primary logo at the top with a gold accent line
- Presentation decks should use the navy/gold/white color palette with Playfair Display headings

### Environmental
- Signage and large-format prints should use the primary logo or monochrome version
- Embossed or engraved applications should use the monochrome version

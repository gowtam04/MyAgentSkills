# Bloom Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview
Bloom is a wellness and meditation startup dedicated to helping people cultivate inner peace, mindfulness, and holistic well-being. The brand represents growth, renewal, and the blossoming of personal potential through guided meditation and wellness practices. Bloom's visual identity draws from nature — specifically the lotus flower — to evoke calm, balance, and transformation.

## Brand Values
- **Mindfulness:** Present-moment awareness is at the heart of everything Bloom offers. The brand communicates intentionality and thoughtful design.
- **Growth:** Like a flower in bloom, the brand represents continuous personal development and the unfolding of potential.
- **Serenity:** Every touchpoint should feel calming and welcoming, creating a sense of peace before the user even begins a session.
- **Accessibility:** Wellness is for everyone. The brand is warm and approachable, never exclusionary or intimidating.

## Logo

### Primary Logo
The primary logo is an abstract lotus flower with layered petals in sage green and soft lavender. It represents growth, balance, and inner calm. Use it as the default mark for Bloom on light backgrounds.

### Logo Variations
| Variation | Use Case | File |
|---|---|---|
| Primary (full color) | Default usage on light backgrounds | `logos/primary-logo.png` |
| Monochrome (black) | Single-color printing, embossing, fax | `logos/monochrome-logo.png` |
| Reversed (white) | Dark backgrounds, photography overlays | `logos/reversed-logo.png` |
| Icon mark | Social profiles, favicons, app icons, small spaces | `logos/icon-mark.png` |

### Logo Usage Rules
- Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the mark
- Minimum display size: 32px height for digital, 0.5" for print
- Do not stretch, rotate, or distort the logo
- Do not change the logo colors outside the approved palette
- Do not place on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast
- Do not add effects (drop shadows, outlines, gradients, bevels)

## Voice & Tone
- **Tone:** Warm and nurturing, yet grounded and genuine. Speaks like a trusted guide, not a guru. Encouraging without being prescriptive.
- **Writing style:** Gentle, clear, and inviting. Favor calm, measured language. Use active voice. Short sentences create breathing room.
- **Avoid:** Clinical or overly medical language, high-pressure urgency, jargon without explanation, overly mystical or new-age cliches.

# Tower Drop — Art, Audio, Juice

## Art direction
Flat pixel art at 2× scale, 16-color palette, dark navy background, warm block colors with a
1 px darker outline. Blocks are 16 px tall.

## Asset list (slice)
- Style lock: palette, rules note, reference sheet.
- Blocks: 4 color variants, plus a "perfect" glow variant.
- Crane: arm and hook, 2-frame sway.
- HUD: digits 0–9, heart icon, Retry button.

## Juice
- Landing: 60 ms hit-stop, 6–10 sparks from the contact edge.
- Perfect drop: white flash on the block and a rising "+N" combo label.
- Miss: the block tumbles off-screen with a short camera shake.

## Audio
Procedural blips for land and perfect; music is a stub.

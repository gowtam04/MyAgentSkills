# Tower Drop — Vision

A one-button stacking game for the browser. A crane swings a block back and forth above the
tower; the player taps to drop it. Overhanging parts are sliced off, so the tower narrows until
the player misses.

## Pillars
- **One verb, one second.** Tap is the only input. A run lasts under two minutes.
- **Readable failure.** The player always sees why a block was trimmed or missed.
- **Crunchy feedback.** Every landing has a hit-stop and a burst of sparks.

## Non-goals
- No meta-progression, shop or accounts in the slice.
- No music in the slice (stub it).

## Vertical slice
Three levels from `data/levels/`, score and lives on the HUD, retry from the results screen.
